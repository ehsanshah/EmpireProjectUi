**Current typed seam to reconcile:** `src/core/contracts.ts` defines the UI types; `src/core/httpClient.ts` proposes `/api/session`, organization capability/project, search, WorkItem, transition, operation, command-by-key and event paths. These are client seam gaps, not evidence of matching server APIs.

**Exit gate:** contract tests and the seven staging journeys above pass with real non-production responses and independent expected results; Demo/Live separation is inspected; failures, permission loss and unknown outcomes are exercised. Until then, the P0 slice is **blocked**.
